Principles

What every page follows. The client asked for a dark, straightforward classic site with less animation; the project rules in CLAUDE.md add the rest.

  • Dark and cool. Graphite grounds and cool white. No cream, beige, yellow or brown anywhere.
  • One accent, used small. The logo lime marks focus, link hover, Saved and the hero slide number. It is never a fill, a band or a button.
  • The client’s own words. Plain section names (Inventory, Sell Your Car, Car Storage, Services, About Us). No eyebrow labels over headings, no invented marketing lines.
  • Calm motion. One ease, no bounce, nothing floats or moves on its own. No autoplay slideshow. Every motion has a reduced-motion state.
  • Descriptions are never hidden. Car descriptions sit open on the VDP, never behind a “read more”.
  • The page looks finished. Placeholders and simulated content are noted in files (data.js, PROVENANCE.md), never labelled on the page.

Color

Values are read live from site.css. Click a name to copy its var(). Ratios are WCAG contrast against the ground named.

Not a token yet: the blue glow rgb(52 124 236) used for hover lines, the Inventory edge line and the ambient corner glow.

Typography

Archivo carries the whole site through its width and weight axes. Sizes are fluid tokens; the pixel value shown is for this window.

--font-ui · ArchivoAa Rr 0968

The only typeface: headings at width 112, body at 100, weights 400 to 600. Google Fonts, variable (wdth 62–125, wght 100–900). The logo is an SVG, not type.

Spacing and layout

An 8px base. Use the steps; never a one-off value.

Radius tokens

Glass

Glass cards sit only over photography: --glass fill, a 16 to 18px backdrop blur, a --glass-line hairline. Never over a flat ground.

Motion

Approved moves only: the hero headline rises line by line, the hero buttons pull in from the right, slide changes swap the headline the same way, sheets slide over pinned sections, the drawer slides in. Everything uses one ease.

--ease over --m-enter
  • Never: bounce, overshoot, floating or drifting photos, pointer-following light, autoplay slideshows.
  • Hover changes colour, border or a light line; nothing lifts.
  • Reduced motion shows the end state at once; Lenis smooth scroll turns off.

Imagery

One photographic style: moody blue-hour campaign. Wet asphalt after rain, a cold steel-blue and graphite grade, high contrast. Cars from the dealer’s eras (’80s, ’90s, 2000s, older classics), no licence plates, no warm tones, no blurred patches. Every generated image is a placeholder, listed in PROVENANCE.md.

Voice and copy

How the site talks. The source is the client: the TikTok bio, the Zoom call and Kirsten’s notes.

  • Plain and direct, like a dealer who loves the cars: “Cars worth getting excited about.” “We buy, sell, trade.”
  • Title Case for section names and buttons as written in the HTML (Sell Your Car, Ask About Storage). CSS sets button labels in uppercase.
  • Numbers as the buyer reads them: $69,900 · 6,188 miles · 6-speed manual. “Call for price” when there is no price.
  • No promises we can’t back: no response times, no “best price”, nothing the client hasn’t said.
  • American English on the page (color, gray); the code comments may differ.
