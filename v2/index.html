    About Us

    We buy, sell, trade cars worth getting excited about — classics, unique daily drivers, oddballs & everything in between.

    Tarmac Motors is a small, independent dealer on North Classen Boulevard in Oklahoma City, run by car people. We keep a short, hand-picked lineup: weekend cars from the ’80s, ’90s and 2000s, a few older classics, oddballs you won’t see twice, and plenty of manuals. We buy cars outright too, and can store yours between drives or send it to our partner shop for coating, PPF or a wrap.

    Driven by passion. Built on trust.
